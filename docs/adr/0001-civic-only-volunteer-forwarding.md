# Civic-only: Volunteers forward Reports, offices never log in

BetterCabanatuan is a volunteer initiative with no agreement with the City Government, so Responsible Offices (City Engineer's Office, CELCOR, DPWH, barangays, City ENRO) get no accounts and no queue in this tool. Volunteers batch Published Reports into a Digest per office and send it through that office's official Citizen's Charter channel: a letter to the Mayor's Office, the text line, or email. They log the send, after which the Reports count as Forwarded. We chose this over an LGU-integrated model (MyNaga, QC iReport) because it lets us launch without City Hall buy-in. It also fits Charter rules that individual residents cannot satisfy themselves: road clearing, de-clogging and streetlight repair require a letter to the Mayor, and streetlight requests are accepted only from barangays or city offices.

## Consequences

- Every status after Forwarded depends on Volunteers learning the outcome. Resolution is a Volunteer judgement, backed by an "after" photo and the Reporter's ability to reopen.
- The Scorecard measures an office from Forwarded onward, so Volunteer delays never count against it.
- If an MOU with the City happens later, giving offices their own accounts is a new feature, not a correction of this model.
